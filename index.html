Look closer at every workout.

SweatQL copies the workouts you record with Apple Watch or the iPhone Fitness app from Apple Health into a database on your iPhone, so you can explore them.

In development, not yet available

Workouts

Every workout, by month.

Browse your whole history grouped by month, and filter it by sport. Each row shows the distance and one more figure for the sport, such as pace or speed.

Workout detail

The numbers, all in one place.

Each workout’s summary stats at a glance: duration, distance, elevation gain, average heart rate, pace and active energy.

Charts and map

See how it went.

A heart-rate chart over time, an elevation profile over distance, and the route on a map.

Totals

Add it all up.

Distance, duration and number of workouts by week or month, stacked by sport.

Your workouts stay on your iPhone.

No accounts, analytics or ads.

There’s nothing to sign in to, and no crash-reporting or tracking software built in.

Read only.

SweatQL reads from Apple Health with your permission. It never writes to it or changes anything in it.

No network requests of its own.

The one use of the network is the route map, where Apple Maps loads tiles for the area shown.

Delete it any time.

Deleting the app deletes its copy of your workouts. Your data in Apple Health isn’t changed.
